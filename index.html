Speak, and it types anywhere.

A dictation keyboard for iPhone and iPad. Talk, tap Stop, and your words appear at the cursor in any app. Speech recognition runs entirely on your device, so nothing is uploaded.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later. No account needed.

A keyboard in every app

Switch to the Dictation keyboard with the globe key, tap the microphone and speak. Your words land where you were typing, in Messages, Mail, Notes or anywhere else.

Nothing leaves your device

NVIDIA's Parakeet speech model runs on your iPhone or iPad. No cloud, no account, no analytics. Audio is deleted as soon as it has been turned into text.

Works offline

Download the speech model once (about 465 MB). After that, dictation works with no connection, on a plane or out of signal.

Every dictation saved

Everything you dictate is kept on your device in Transcripts, ready to search, copy or share.
